Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how NeonRushLive ("we", "us"), an independent creator based in London, United Kingdom, handles information on this website and in the NeonRushLive Worker, a private software tool we use to turn our own gameplay streams into short videos for our own channels.

1. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or forms, and does not collect personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

2. Who uses the NeonRushLive Worker

The Worker is used only by NeonRushLive to manage NeonRushLive's own accounts. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, members of the public.

3. Information the Worker accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): our own channel's basic details, uploads of our own videos, the processing status of those videos, and our own channel analytics (for example views, average percentage viewed, likes and shares).
  • Twitch: our own past broadcasts and clips, to find highlights.

The Worker does not collect information about viewers, commenters or any other people.

4. How we use and store it

  • Our own analytics are used only to decide what kinds of clips to make next. They are visible only to NeonRushLive, are never sold or shared, and YouTube data is never combined with data from other platforms.
  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in our private GitHub repository and are never shown, shared or published.
  • Draft videos and processing records are kept in our private repository.

5. YouTube API Services

The Worker uses YouTube API Services. By using it, NeonRushLive agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Information obtained from YouTube is handled in line with this policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deletion

Access granted to the Worker can be revoked at any time through Google security settings (YouTube) or Twitch Connections settings. When access is revoked, or on request, stored API data is deleted within 30 days.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information obtained through the Worker with anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
